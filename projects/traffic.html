Case study 03 · Transportation / road safety

Traffic Accident Analysis

An analysis of 209,275 traffic crash records using injury rates alongside counts to compare road, weather and lighting conditions.

MySQLSQLPower BIDAX

Business problem

Crash counts alone can be misleading because some conditions occur more frequently than others. This project compares both counts and rates to identify conditions associated with greater injury risk.

Key findings

Weather: Clear weather had the most total injuries, while Rain had the highest injury rate at about 41.2 injuries per 100 crashes.
Lighting: Daylight had the most incapacitating injuries, while Darkness, lighted road had the highest incapacitating-injury rate.
Road surface: Dry roads had the most crashes, and Unable to determine was the most common primary cause within dry-road crashes.
Crash type: Turning was the most common first crash type.

Recommendations

Prioritize safety measures around higher-risk conditions such as Rain and Darkness, lighted road; improve crash-cause recording; and investigate Turning crashes, particularly at intersections.

Limitations

This is observational data, so findings show associations rather than causal relationships. Traffic-volume data was unavailable, and 2013 and 2025 are partial years.

Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard covers weather, lighting, road surface, crash type, contributory cause, injury rates and monthly patterns.
