About

Data analyst with a practical mindset.

I’m a self-taught data analyst. I come from a laboratory science background with no formal data training, and I learned SQL, Power BI and Excel on my own through the Alex the Analyst bootcamp and by building projects from raw data to finished dashboard. Learning this way taught me to check my own work. I look at the data before I draw the chart, decide what counts as a duplicate, and say plainly what the numbers can and cannot prove. Every project here lists its limitations for that reason. I’m looking for a junior data analyst role where I can keep learning and work with SQL and Power BI every day.

What I bring

My portfolio demonstrates hands-on work in data preparation, SQL analysis, Power BI visualization, documentation and analytical problem solving.

I am particularly interested in roles where I can work with operational, financial, customer or business data and contribute to better decisions through evidence.
